# PROSE

Raksha is found alone, the last survivor of a destroyed civilization. Tuwak’inna stands over him, radiating quiet authority. They share a moment of profound realization about an ancient inheritance. Then, a rapid montage: the merging of five powers into the RUNIC Federation; military conflict erupting on Earth; the stark reality of mass displacement; humanity fleeing under duress. Transition to Mars, where an artifact pulses with light. The emergence of 'new Aryren' is shown as subtle cognitive shifts in human forms. Finally, a desperate exodus: Arks preparing for departure, Awakened figures leaving the Solar System boundary.

---

# ENVIRONMENT

Vast, desolate ruins of a destroyed civilization; sterile, cold Federation architecture; the red dust and stark landscapes of Mars; the deep void of space; the interior of massive preservation vessels (arks).

---

# MOOD AND LIGHTING

Initial mood is heavy, melancholic, and profound, shifting to determined and urgent. Lighting is often stark and high-contrast—cold blues and harsh shadows emphasizing isolation and existential threat. Later scenes feature a sense of desperate hope mixed with impending doom, illuminated by the glow of ancient artifacts.

---

# CHARACTERS

Tuwak’inna (Age indeterminate, strong presence): Calm, resolute, embodying leadership. Raksha (Age indeterminate, survivor): Weary, haunted eyes, conveying deep loss. Xavier and GEID Star (AI representations): Abstract forms of immense, cold intelligence. The Awakened: A collective group radiating subtle, heightened awareness.
